2. Georgia State University, HyperPhysics, *Precession of Spinning Top*. https://www.hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/top.html
3. University of Illinois Physics Van, *Spinning Tops*. https://van.physics.illinois.edu/ask/listing/308
4. University of Rochester, *Rolling, Torque and Angular Momentum*. https://teacher.pas.rochester.edu/phy121/lecturenotes/Chapter12/Chapter12.html

教材採定性說明：重力作用於偏離接觸點的重心，對支點產生力矩；力矩改變角動量方向並形成進動。摩擦與空氣阻力使轉速降低，之後晃動、進動與傾倒更明顯。未將「離心力」當成唯一原因，也未宣稱旋轉抵消重力。
